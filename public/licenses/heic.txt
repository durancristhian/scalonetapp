HEIC photo conversion notices

Scaloneta uses heic-to 1.6.6, by Hopper Gee, distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.
It includes libheif 1.23.6 and libde265 1.0.16, under LGPL-3.0-or-later.
The library is used without modifications. It is loaded separately, on demand,
in a browser worker. No photo is sent to a conversion service.
